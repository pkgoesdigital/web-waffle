## Safety Incidents

### Garage Theft Uptick — South End of City Park West (flagged June 1)
DPD District 2 Command alerted the neighborhood to an increase in garage thefts on the south end of City Park West. Both open garages and locked side-person entry doors are being targeted; bikes, tools, and other items have been taken. DPD's recommended precautions: close garage doors, lock side entry doors, register bikes (with serial numbers), and photograph/inventory tools.

## Resident Observations and Themes

Resident-facing communications this month came from daily Gmail/Nextdoor digests and, newly incorporated this revision, the "High and Race St Safety" WhatsApp group. Observed themes:

- **Garage and property security remains top of mind.** DPD's own repeated messaging (garage thefts, bike registration, vehicle valuables) tracks with the pattern flagged in May's recap — this appears to be a sustained, not one-off, concern for the south end of the neighborhood.
- **DPD giveaway events are landing well with residents.** On WhatsApp (June 15), Paula shared a firsthand account of attending a DPD giveaway event — a free steering-wheel CLUB, a free AirTag (confirmed Android-compatible via Samsung SmartThings, after a resident asked), and plans to register her bike at DPD's bike-recovery garage. Residents expressed interest in a repeat event if demand is there.
- **Storm damage prompted a wave of mutual-aid recommendations, not safety concerns.** A June 1 hailstorm ("ping pong ball sized," per one resident) prompted a multi-day WhatsApp thread comparing roofers and auto hail-repair shops — a community-resilience data point, not a security matter.
- **A tree-hazard safety note surfaced informally.** On June 8, a resident (Beth) posted photos of storm-damaged, dangling tree limbs and warned neighbors to be careful where they park, noting several trees on the block have similar issues. Not DPD-relevant, but worth relaying to Parks/Forestry if not already reported.
- **TGP Good Neighbor Committee coordination continues, and the group grew slightly.** The June 24 meeting covered gate/security and lighting updates, a DPD briefing, and an open question about whether neighbors (e.g., a contact from the Hinds office) are still missing from GNC distribution.
