## Summary

- July was a quiet month for direct safety incidents in City Park West itself. Most Nextdoor-flagged items (Cherry Creek Reservoir drownings, a SE Denver nightclub shooting, a car-vandalism teaser, a "waitress" post) were citywide or other-neighborhood stories with no confirmed City Park West nexus.
- DPD District 2 announced **National Night Out — Tuesday, August 4th, 6–8pm, at City Park Pavilion** (2001 Steele St), with first responder vehicles, K-9s, a climbing wall, and free food. Worth a final push to residents ahead of the date.
- An HOA thread for 1532 High St (immediately adjacent to The Gathering Place) documented repeated unauthorized use of an exterior water spigot — filling containers and bathing — tied to increased foot traffic from TGP, plus a prior flooding incident. The board approved a lockbox and repairs.
- East Colfax BRT construction continued to affect the corridor: overnight milling (July 12–13, Broadway–Sherman) and rolling daytime intersection closures (July 14–22, Sherman–Race).

## Safety Incidents

### DPD Crime Prevention Tune-Up & Giveaway — Carla Madison Rec Center (July 2)
DPD District 2, Denver Park Rangers, Carla Madison Rec, and City Council District 10 co-hosted a crime-prevention event with free ice cream, steering-wheel locks, AirTag/DenverTrack registration, and 529 Garage bike registration with a free QR decal. A good touchpoint for future promotion — worth flagging the next occurrence to residents earlier in the month.

---

### Water Spigot Misuse / TGP-Adjacent Property Impact — 1532 High St (July 9–12)
An HOA board member reported that increased foot traffic from The Gathering Place across the street has repeatedly driven people to an unlocked exterior water spigot at 1532 High St to fill containers and bathe. A prior incident where someone left the spigot running caused a flooded basement. The board approved installing a security lockbox (Work Order #12230) plus related entryway drywall/light-fixture and stucco repairs; a vendor (Indispensable Services) was engaged as of July 10. This is HOA maintenance business, not a DPD case, but it's a concrete, dated data point on how TGP-adjacent foot traffic is affecting the block.

### Trash-Scavenging Incident, Condo Building (July 18)
A resident reported that a condo building's trash bins had been rummaged through overnight and reported it to 311. A Neighborhood Watch member spoke directly with the person doing the scavenging and asked him to put items back; by evening the area had been cleaned up.

### Recurring Loose-Dog Traffic Hazard — High St (July 19–20)
A small dog was seen roaming loose near High St and 16th Ave, prompting resident concern; it has reportedly nearly been hit by cars on multiple occasions. Residents identified the dog as belonging to a nearby address and said the owner has been notified more than once without a lasting fix, and it periodically returns home on its own. Residents suggested a formal animal-control report given the repeat near-misses and unresponsive owner.

### Alley Trash Dumping & Open-Flame Incident — Race/High Alley (July 29)

## Resident Observations and Themes

Resident-facing communications this month came from daily Gmail/Nextdoor digests and the "High and Race St Safety" WhatsApp group. Observed themes:

- **TGP-adjacent property impact remains a recurring, dated theme.** The spigot-misuse incident at 1532 High St echoes prior months' concerns about foot traffic and property strain near The Gathering Place — a concrete, dated example that has previously supported DPD asks.
- **Alley disorder continues at a low but steady grade.** Trash dumping in the Race/High alley recurred within the same day it was cleaned, and a resident intervened to stop someone attempting to light material on fire amid drought conditions (July 29) — consistent with the pattern flagged in prior months.
- **Day-to-day resident reports skew toward lower-level property and alley disorder rather than major incidents.** The WhatsApp export showed a DPD safety giveaway at Carla Madison Rec Center (July 2), a same-day-resolved trash-scavenging incident (July 18), and open drug paraphernalia observed near the alley disorder discussion (July 19) — useful context for calibrating the group's day-to-day traffic against DPD's own bulletins.
- **An animal-control gap surfaced.** A loose dog with a known but unresponsive owner caused repeat near-miss vehicle incidents on High St (July 19–20); residents want a formal animal-control reporting path.
- **Corridor construction (East Colfax BRT) continues to roll through the neighborhood**, with overlapping night and day work windows in July — residents should be kept informed, especially since a similar June notice was later corrected from night to day work.
- **Mutual aid remains active.** Neighbors coordinated informally on porch-piracy prevention, including holding a package for a traveling household.
- **The distribution boundary question is now open.** In a Gmail exchange (July 1–3), a neighboring business (Scott, Dauphine Hotel) asked whether the newsletter's coverage should extend toward Vine St, and asked to join the distribution list. Paula clarified that resident reports come in exclusively via the WhatsApp group and agreed to add Scott to this month's distribution — the boundary question itself should be resolved before it recurs again.
