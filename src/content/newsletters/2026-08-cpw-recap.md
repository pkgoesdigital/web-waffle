### Backpack & School Supply Giveaway — Skyland Park (Aug 8)
District 2 held a giveaway, 10am–noon at Skyland Park (3380 N Holly St). No incident reports tied to the event.

### High St Bike Theft & Same-Day Partial Recovery (Aug 8)
A resident's bike was stolen despite an industrial chain lock securing the front wheel to the frame — it hadn't been anchored to a fixed object, so the thief took the whole bike. It was logged with Denver Bicycle Garage/Project529 and located later the same day nearby, missing only a rear wheel, apparently abandoned after the thief couldn't get far. Residents were reminded to register bikes through Project529.

---

### Suspicious Vehicle, Suspected Dealing & Vehicle Impound (Aug 8)
Residents reported an individual near a High St address associated with suspected drug activity, seen with bolt cutters, along with a blue SUV displaying fake temporary plates; multiple neighbors called it in and DPD responded, logging the vehicle for referral to an undercover unit. Later the same day, a separate wellness check in the area led officers to the same vehicle, where an occupant was found with narcotics and in medical distress; she was hospitalized and the vehicle was impounded, with residents noting signs consistent with a stolen vehicle.

---

### Property Hardening — La Paz Townhomes (Aug 8)
In response to repeated break-in attempts, the La Paz townhomes added anti-theft hardware (anti-climb spikes and steel plates shielding door locks from being pried open from outside).

### Organized Bike Theft Activity (Aug 23)
Two theft incidents surfaced in the same week: an e-bike was stolen from a resident's backyard overnight, and separately, camera footage showed what residents described as a bike-theft group temporarily stashing a stolen bike in another yard — after also taking a front gate lock — before returning hours later to retrieve it, suggesting coordinated rather than opportunistic activity. Residents also reported that DPD's online non-emergency portal is no longer accepting trespassing reports, though District 2 confirmed it will still take them by phone. ## Resident Observations and Themes

Resident-facing communications this month came from daily Gmail/Nextdoor digests and the "High and Race St Safety" WhatsApp export, which showed meaningfully more activity than DPD's own emails captured this month:

- **Property crime showed signs of organization, not just opportunism.** A High St bike theft was partially recovered the same day (Aug 8); an e-bike theft and a separate incident — camera footage of a group temporarily stashing a stolen bike in another yard after taking a front gate lock — surfaced in the same week (Aug 23), consistent with prior months' "bicycle chop shop" concerns.
- **A suspected drug/dealing stop escalated quickly.** An individual with bolt cutters near a suspicious vehicle with fake temp plates was reported and logged for referral to an undercover unit; hours later a separate wellness check on the same vehicle found an occupant hospitalized for narcotics-related medical distress, and the vehicle was impounded (Aug 8).
- **A possible attempted vehicular assault raised public-safety concern.** Residents described an overnight incident in the alley near 16th & Race as someone attempting to run another person over; DPD requested camera/audio footage (Aug 16).
- **Residents are hardening their own properties.** La Paz townhomes added anti-climb spikes and pry-resistant door hardware after repeated break-in attempts (Aug 8).
- **A reporting-channel gap needs DPD confirmation.** Residents report DPD's online non-emergency portal no longer accepts trespassing reports, pushing volume to phone lines — worth verifying directly with DPD.
- **DPD repeated the same bulletins across two emails this month** (Aug 4 and Aug 20 both listed the Verbena Park, Syracuse St, and E Colfax items) — may indicate a pattern District 2 wants residents to stay aware of, rather than new activity.
- **The Neighborhood Watch group is actively expanding through the RNO board network, but hit an administrative bottleneck.** Via the "Coffee Klatch Follow Up" Gmail thread, Scott H. connected new board member Zac H. with Paula; Zac supplied four new contacts (Zachary H., Tara M., Charles "Mack" V., and Jane J.) on Aug 24, but two neighbors (Holly & Jo) are still not added to WhatsApp, and Paula is traveling through October — she plans to request WhatsApp admin access to add people directly and run another flyering round on her return.
- **The distribution boundary question (Vine St/Gilpin) is unresolved for a second month** and should be settled before it recurs again.
- **East Colfax BRT corridor activity continued on two tracks** — ongoing construction impacts (overnight work near Colorado Blvd, Aug 16–20) alongside business-support programming (Fax Pass Pop-up, Mural Festival, and the Sept 10 briefing).
- Also noted: an encampment report of roughly five people behind a residential garage (Aug 18), with no drugs observed.
